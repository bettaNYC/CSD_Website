Services

Design that starts with the place.

From interiors to exteriors to the building itself, every service is drawn from a project's location, history, and surroundings.

From interior architecture and custom millwork to complete residential, hospitality, and retail environments, each project is developed with a thoughtful response to its context, architecture, and the people who inhabit it.

  • Interior DesignThoughtful, highly tailored interiors that bring together architecture, materiality, furnishings, art, and light.
  • Custom DesignBespoke millwork, furniture, and architectural elements developed specifically for each project.
  • ArchitectureArchitectural solutions that balance context, proportion, materiality, and the way people experience a space.
  • Hospitality & RetailDistinctive environments shaped around brand, experience, function, and a strong sense of place.

The studio

Every project starts outside the comfort zone — treated as a new theme to explore.

Carmelina Santoro is an Italian architect trained at Peter Marino Architect and Vignelli Associates, with a Ph.D. from the Politecnico di Torino. Her approach draws inspiration from each project's specific location, its history, and its geographical surroundings.

Meet Carmelina

Design Philosophy

Every project begins with a story.

The architecture, the setting, the people, the objects they collect, and the way they live all become part of the design. I bring these elements together through material, light, color, and custom detail to create spaces that feel considered, individual, and connected to their surroundings.

  • New YorkUrban residences and luxury retail environments, where architecture, craftsmanship, and contemporary living meet.
  • Miami / South BeachResidential and hospitality interiors informed by the city’s architecture, light, color, and cultural character.
  • CaribbeanHospitality environments rooted in local materials, landscape, and the relaxed character of island life.
I love for people who see my designs to remember the experience as comfortable and soothing — speaking to their soul.
Carmelina Santoro, Founder
