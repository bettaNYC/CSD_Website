Location
Grenada, West Indies
Category
Villas

Laluna Ville is a group of five private villas on the island of Grenada, in the West Indies — each with a different layout, distributed along the cliff between the tropical garden and the open views of the Caribbean coast, with indoor rooms opening onto shaded terraces and pool decks built for the climate.
