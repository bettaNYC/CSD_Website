About me

Carmelina Santoro

An Italian architect from Turin, with a Ph.D. in Architecture from the Politecnico di Torino.

In 1988 Carmelina moved to New York, where she interned at Bentley LaRosa Salasky Design and Vignelli Associates before joining Peter Marino Architect — the practice behind flagship stores for many of the world's leading luxury houses.

She went on to become Store Planning Manager for a roster of international fashion and jewellery maisons, shaping retail environments across the United States.

Alongside her work in luxury retail, Carmelina has developed a portfolio of high-end residential interiors in New York and Miami, creating refined, highly tailored environments with a focus on architectural detail, materiality, and a strong sense of place. In some of her more recent residential projects, including the Classic Apartment, she has collaborated with freelance designer Diamante Pedersoli, bringing together their complementary approaches to design and interiors.

Her hospitality portfolio includes the Laluna Resort in Grenada, the Betsy Hotel on Ocean Drive in Miami, completed in 2009, and design work for The Carlton in South Beach.
